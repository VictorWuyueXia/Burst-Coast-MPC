# Rotary-Pendulum PWA Plan: Interpretation Summary

## Recommendation

Approximate the existing discrete RK4 map directly, rather than linearizing the
continuous equations and introducing a second discretization mismatch.

The first model uses 540 local affine regions over wrapped pendulum angle, both angular
velocities, and torque. Rotary-arm angle is not partitioned because it does not appear
in the dynamics; its cable limit remains an explicit validity constraint.

## Structure

Each region stores

$$
x^+=A_rx+B_ru+c_r,
$$

where the matrices are exact derivatives of the symbolic RK4 graph at the region
anchor. Region selection is deterministic, vectorized, and periodic in pendulum angle.
States outside the declared velocity or arm domain are rejected explicitly.

Only four objects are planned: configuration, immutable affine region, vectorized
piecewise-affine dynamics, and artifact writer. Five substantive operations cover
construction, selection, prediction, auditing, and artifact finalization.

## Validation judgment

The nonlinear RK4 model remains the reference at every milestone. The plan checks:

- symbolic Jacobians against finite differences;
- complete and unique region ownership;
- exact anchor reproduction and equilibrium preservation;
- stratified one-step error;
- jumps across adjacent region faces;
- 250-step nonlinear-versus-affine rollout divergence;
- later closed-loop action replay on the nonlinear plant.

The initial acceptance target is below 1% normalized one-step RMSE, below 3% at the
95th percentile, and below 10% worst case. These are model-quality thresholds, not
physical safety tolerances.

## Next decision

If local accuracy passes but boundary jumps fail, keep the partition and impose
continuity during affine fitting. If local accuracy also fails, refine only the audited
worst angle/velocity intervals. Do not add neural residuals or mixed-integer control
until the affine bank itself passes.
