# How to read the figure plan

The [outline](outline.md) specifies five main figures and an optional sixth. Figures 1 and 4 are wide double-column page-top figures. Figures 2 and 3 are single-column figures; Figure 2 is about half a page high. Figure 5 is the planned wide scenario/baseline resource atlas; Figure 6 optionally compares conventional control metrics.

Figures 1–4 have final local reproduction packages. Use the [figure index](../interpretation_summary.md) to find the retained source, final exports and run commands. The energy and sensitivity plots use verified historical heuristic records. The session figure uses one continuously enabled MPC+RL record; its act-coast comparison remains empty. Final act-coast and the requested matched baseline comparisons require future records.

Interpret total computation time as total online controller computation time. Keep actuator activations, enabled time, replanning events, nonzero input support and nested computation timers distinct. Apply the [general plotting rules](../figure_style_rules.md) and each figure's narrative contract during manual refinement.
