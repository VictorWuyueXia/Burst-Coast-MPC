# Hamiltonian controller implementation contract

## Plant and phase

Use the state order `x = [theta, alpha, omega, nu]` from `jax_dynamics.py`. Arm position θ is unwrapped and pendulum position α is stored on [−π, π). Their time derivatives ω and ν are in rad/s. Shaft torque `u` is in N·m. Derive the base inertia Jᵦ, pendulum pivot inertia Jₚ, coupling inertia J꜀, and gravity coefficient G from the existing `ModelConstants`. The damping coefficients bₐ and bₚ are those of the active physical model. Use `heuristic.energy.encode` to obtain the nominal Hamiltonian H in joules by summing the physical energy components. Use `dynamics.energy_components` as the reference. The quantities A and c below are the rotary inertia and cross inertia. The cyclic momentum p has units kg·m²/s and describes the arm's momentum including the pendulum coupling. The overdots denote derivatives with respect to simulation time in seconds.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A(\alpha)&=J_b+J_p\sin^2\alpha, & c(\alpha)&=J_c\cos\alpha,\\
p&=A(\alpha)\omega+c(\alpha)\nu, & \dot p&=u-b_a\omega,\\
\dot H&=u\omega-b_a\omega^2-b_p\nu^2, & H^\star&=2G.
\end{aligned}
$$

For the energy-feature diagnostic, let Jₐ be the arm's own inertia from the model. The energy terms Kₐ, K꜀ and Kₚ are computed from the observed state. They denote arm-body kinetic energy, carried and coupled kinetic contribution, and pendulum-pivot kinetic energy, respectively. Vₚ is gravitational potential relative to downward. K꜀ is signed. The diagnostic groups are `(K_a, K_c + K_p, V_p)`, `(K_a, K_c, K_p + V_p)` and `(K_a, K_c, K_p, V_p)`. Each group sums to H and omits the absolute arm angle. Compute these groups in the invariant tests.

$$
K_a=\tfrac12 J_a\omega^2,\quad K_c=\tfrac12(A-J_a)\omega^2+c\omega\nu,\quad
K_p=\tfrac12J_p\nu^2,\quad V_p=G(1-\cos\alpha).
$$

For the torque-free undamped model, H and p label a reduced energy level. The reduced inertia D is positive. The effective potential Vₑ includes the momentum-dependent kinetic contribution. The reconstruction for ω recovers the instantaneous arm speed and the reduced energy identity determines the magnitude of ν.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(\alpha)&=J_p-c(\alpha)^2/A(\alpha),\\
V_e(\alpha;p)&=G(1-\cos\alpha)+p^2/(2A(\alpha)),\\
H&=\tfrac12 D(\alpha)\nu^2+V_e(\alpha;p),\\
\omega&=(p-c(\alpha)\nu)/A(\alpha).
\end{aligned}
$$

Identify the connected component of the allowed angles containing α. Bracket turning points in the monotone segments of the effective potential. The chart identifies the well for a libration and the direction for a rotation. The sign of ν selects the leg within a libration chart and can change at a turn. To find the monotone segments, use `z = cos(alpha)` on [−1, 1]. The potential is convex in z and has at most one interior minimum in z. Map its critical and turning values back to both angle signs. Test the two wells at high momentum, plus the symmetric libration and both rotation directions. This prevents two disjoint orbits with identical H and p from sharing a phase origin.

Let a denote an integration angle. The time integrand q(a) is the reciprocal reduced speed. In a libration with adjacent turning angles a₋ and a₊, the period T is twice the positive-direction flight time between the turns. The variable ℓ is elapsed time since leaving a₋ toward increasing α, including the positive leg on the return. Use an endpoint-removing substitution `a = a_minus + (a_plus - a_minus) sin²(s)` with integration coordinate s from 0 to π/2. Use the continuous limiting phase at each simple turning point. For rotations, integrate q over one full revolution, with elapsed time measured from α = −π in the direction of ν. Phase φ is measured in radians and advances in either rotation direction. Use lifted phase only within a consistent orbit chart. In nominal conservative coast, verify uniform phase advance against the full ODE.

$$
q(a)=\sqrt{\frac{D(a)}{2[H-V_e(a;p)]}},\qquad
T=2\int_{a_-}^{a_+}q(a)\,da\quad\text{(libration)},\qquad
\phi=2\pi\ell/T\pmod{2\pi}.
$$

Report physical exclusions at reduced equilibria and near critical levels of the current connected component. A float64 root or quadrature failure on a classified regular orbit is an execution error. Validate phase across chart boundaries and reset the event latch on a chart change or excluded sample. Require a fresh observed window entry before rearming, so an ambiguous chart transition cannot produce a duplicate pulse. As a separate check on the role of p, log arm displacement over a conservative cycle. The reconstructed contribution proportional to ν integrates to zero over a complete libration or rotation. The remaining displacement equals p times the integral of 1/A over the cycle. The pendulum can therefore repeat its orbit while the arm drifts.

## Candidate selection and execution

1. At each 20 ms supervisor tick, update orbit and local-capture status from the observed state. In a regular chart, use the current T to set the half- and full-period horizons. Round each horizon up to a supervisor tick and cap it at two natural small-oscillation periods rounded up to a tick. On a physically excluded level use half and full natural periods for the arm forecast by design. The natural period is `MODEL.natural_period_s`. Predict with `jax_dynamics.rk4_step` using 20 substeps per tick for 1 ms samples and include the initial state. For the arm maximum, check the substep nodes and interpolated extrema where arm speed changes sign. The limit is 135° and the initial numerical clearance is 0.5°. Execute and audit using 40 substeps per tick for 0.5 ms samples. Treat disagreements at a classification boundary as unresolved. The experiment owns its session clock and capture scoring so an angle-only success latch cannot stop the trial.
2. Before scheduling energy, screen coast. If coast fails on the half horizon, evaluate brake immediately. If only the full horizon fails, allow a screened, energy-improving pulse in an eligible window and otherwise assess brake. Brake uses saturated feedback to request arm acceleration `−brake_gain * omega` using the drift acceleration at zero torque and the positive input coefficient `Jp / (A * Jp - c**2)`. Recompute the brake command at each predicted tick. Retain the brake until both coast horizons clear twice the nominal margin. Brake can interrupt any powered mode. If no admissible candidate in the declared set exists, record `no_screened_action` and terminate the episode. This status concerns the candidate set and screen, since untested inputs may exist.
3. Use the two windows centered at π/2 and 3π/2, with an initial half-width of π/6. Offer one launch at a fresh window entry, including a regular reset that begins in a window. Record a consumed entry even if every pulse is rejected. Do not replay missed windows between ticks. Enumerate a symmetric 17-point torque grid including zero at the inherited cap and positive pulse durations of one through five ticks, restricted to the available horizon. For each candidate, simulate its pulse followed by coast. Retain only candidates whose predicted powered segment stays in the same valid window at its control knots. Before applying any candidate torque, also check the upcoming tick at 0.5 ms samples for phase or chart exits that occur between the knots. Repeat these checks for active pulses before continuing. Record whether each executed pulse straddled its center. This is window-entry timing, with exact centering deferred.
4. Let ΔHᵍ be the fractional target from the development plan. For a candidate of duration τ, compute its delivered energy ΔH from the difference between H at the predicted pulse end and the observed H. Compare the residual magnitude `abs(ΔH - ΔH_g)` to the coast residual at the same τ. Require improvement by at least `1e-4 * H_star` and a strictly smaller absolute upright energy error than at the start. Among admissible candidates minimize the residual, then prefer shorter duration and smaller torque magnitude. Freeze a deterministic tie order for equal magnitudes. Log net energy change and motor work separately, including brake or window interruptions. Cyclic momentum is a diagnostic in the nominal pulse policy.
5. At exact downward rest permit one declared positive-cap launch of 20 ms, screened as launch then coast. Detect exact rest from the reset or measured downward equilibrium state. A rejected launch is reported explicitly. A reduced equilibrium other than this launch state, or a point near a separatrix, has no orbital pulse eligibility. Coast and braking remain defined there. The local capture guard takes precedence over phase eligibility. Count an uncaptured stall as a failure at timeout.
6. Use the existing 100 ms LQR design at the matched cap. The historical teacher settings file referenced by `hybrid_run.py` is absent in this checkout. Reconstruct the gain from the documented weights and the current 100 ms discrete map with `solve_discrete_are`. Require pole magnitudes below one and agreement with the documented pole magnitudes to absolute tolerance `1e-5` at the documented integration resolution. Define the wrapped upright error `beta = atan2(-sin(alpha), -cos(alpha))`. Require one continuous second inside the `mission.yaml` box with `abs(theta) < 0.08 rad`, `abs(beta) < 5°`, `abs(omega) < 0.15 rad/s` and `abs(nu) < 0.20 rad/s`. Release at twice any entry limit. These are proposed handoff thresholds. Verify entry trajectories under nonlinear feedback and record the region actually tested. Hold each LQR command for five supervisor ticks unless the state exits the release box or the supervisor selects a brake. Screen the scheduled saturated local feedback over the prediction intervals, including any residual hold time. Reset capture dwell on a brake interruption or release.

## Structure budget

Use array batches for starts, independent orbit samples, and candidate torques and durations. Use scans for integration and the causal update sequence. Keep one-line comments at the key code blocks. Keep task-oriented files and functions in the repository's preferred length ranges. Keep orchestration and output formatting in the experiment runner. The plan allocates four implementation files and one test file.

| File | Functions and responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| `src/rotary_pendulum/heuristic/orbital_phase.py` | `orbital_invariants(x, physical, model)` computes H and p from the plant and reused energy routines. `orbital_phase(x, H, p, model)` classifies the connected component, solves for turning angles and returns chart status, T and φ. |
| `src/rotary_pendulum/heuristic/orbital_prediction.py` | `predict_candidates(x, schedules, horizons, plant)` batches existing RK4 rollouts, propagates scheduled brake or LQR commands where applicable and returns states, endpoint energy and half/full arm extrema. |
| `src/rotary_pendulum/heuristic/orbital_controller.py` | `controller_step(x, memory, plant, tuning, variant)` advances the event latch and executes the priority logic. Returns the next torque, controller memory and a flat diagnostic record. |
| `scripts/experiments/hamiltonian/run.py` | `evaluate(starts, variant, tuning, output)` reconstructs the LQR once, runs paired episodes, audits executed controls, and writes the experimental record. Use the existing baseline runners for native baseline outputs. |
| `tests/test_orbital_controller.py` | `test_invariants`, `test_regular_orbits`, `test_pulse_events`, `test_screen_and_brake`, `test_launch_and_capture` cover the corresponding milestones with parametrized cases. |

Use one new `OrbitalMemory` record for mode, chart and window latch, pulse torque and ticks remaining, launch-used flag, capture dwell, and held LQR torque and ticks remaining. Use arrays and existing records for state, model, candidate trajectories and diagnostics. Do not add a class hierarchy, additional data classes, wrappers, CLI modes, configuration domains or fallback control paths. Use explicit experiment inputs and let invalid numeric states fail visibly. `starts`, `variant`, `tuning` and `output` are independent experiment inputs. The dynamics evolve `x` and controller memory. The independent pulse search variables are torque and integer duration. Half and full horizons, orbital phase, and candidate scores are computed quantities.

Expose three new tunables in the experiment specification only. Start with `k_energy = 0.35`, `phase_half_width = π/6` rad and `brake_gain = 8 s⁻¹`. These are engineering trial values. Hold the cap, arm bound, control and integration clocks, torque grid, pulse duration grid, launch, clearance, energy deadband, and capture predicates fixed for matched trials. The default critical-energy exclusion is `1e-5 * H_star` from relevant critical levels. Treat it as a fixed numerical domain rule and verify rejection and adjacent regular-orbit convergence before the controller campaign.

## Validation and experimental records

- **Physics and phase.** In float64, require energy and momentum rate identities within `1e-8` relative error after scaling by representative rates from the upright energy and momentum scales divided by the natural period. Use derivative evaluations for both torque signs and nonzero damping. Verify conservative cycle drift below `1e-5` relative to the same energy and momentum scales. Require period agreement within 0.5% of independently refined full-ODE returns to the same directed section and circular phase error below 0.02 rad away from the declared exclusions. Include simple turning points, disconnected wells, both signed rotations and angle wrapping. Compare physical energy feature sums to `energy_components()` and include opposite velocity states with equal H and different power at nonzero torque.
- **Execution.** Compare planned and executed energy changes and arm peaks on both signs of torque. Check the one-launch-per-window rule, cap, normal hold duration, brake interruption, release hysteresis, and terminal status. Include an interior arm peak with safe horizon endpoints. Require no accepted-to-violating reversals in the refined replay of the validation cases. Count starts already outside the screening margin separately. Check saved LQR weights, poles and a nonlinear capture/release replay at the benchmark cap.
- **Paired variants.** Replay the 32 official starts at the frozen benchmark settings and add a separately reported fixed stress set. Run official MPC+RL, the existing heuristic at the matched cap, a dense energy-momentum diagnostic, ungated screened pulses, gated unscreened pulses and gated screened pulses. The dense diagnostic uses the same supervisor and evaluates the torque grid at every 20 ms update. It scores the one-tick endpoint by the squared upright energy error plus squared momentum, each divided by the square of its own scale. Its energy scale is H★ and its momentum scale is `sqrt(2 * Jb * H_star)`, the pure-arm momentum at target energy at a vertical pendulum angle. The ungated pulse ablation removes only phase eligibility and re-arms after each scheduled pulse ends. It retains launch, braking and local capture. The unscreened ablation removes arm rejection and braking. Keep the phase, torque and capture rules. Preserve the heuristic's policy and change only its run cap for the primary comparison. Compare the heuristic's historical native-cap results separately. In separate stress replays use the parameterized NumPy plant to test small nonzero damping and a 5% increase in pendulum mass, with nominal controller settings frozen. For the damping case use `0.05 * Jb * 2π/T_nat` and `0.05 * Jp * 2π/T_nat` for rotary and pendulum damping, where `T_nat` is the model's natural small-oscillation period.
- **Tuning and judgment.** On a separate 8-start development set, vary one of the three design tunables at a time, then repeat with the best settings if tradeoffs remain. Rank by full-state capture, unresolved arm screens and excursions before powered time and absolute work. Freeze one setting before the confirmation runs. Treat an early `no_screened_action` termination as a failed 40 s trial. Report the observed trajectory length and a composite count of arm violations or unresolved screens alongside raw crossing counts. Use paired differences to assess gating. Only claim a benefit if the gated version improves powered time or work without worsening measured capture or the composite arm outcome on the paired set. Stop to diagnose repeated stalls, arm-screen rejections or capture releases by mode and orbit class. Revisit the formulation if tuning no longer improves those outcomes.
- **Records.** Save source identifiers, start arrays, resolved design, ordered command traces, 0.5 ms states, candidate and rejection summaries, phase and mode events, energy and work balances, and timings under `artifacts/rotary_pendulum/hamiltonian/<run>/machine-scannables/`. Use per-episode status and metrics CSV plus array traces. Report the first one-second full-state capture, hold duration, and the official angle-only score as separate metrics. Report raw and composite arm outcomes, signed and absolute motor work in joules, powered seconds and rising-edge switch-ons, phase alignment, torque saturation, braking, and time per decision apart from compilation. Save figures and `interpretation_summary.md` under the run's `human-readables/`. Print batch progress during long runs. Start on CPU. For later GPU batches check occupancy before launch, prefer idle devices 6–7 and stop if more than four GPUs are occupied.
