# Figure 2: rotary-pendulum benchmark

[Edit Figure 2 in Canva](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHZQCCIN6M/XlPL47W5PJUg8DRToWfg4A/edit).

The shaded 3D apparatus makes the servo base, vertical shaft, driven arm, passive hinge and pendulum recognizable. Its original vector construction follows the benchmark's link geometry and uses [Quanser's official apparatus imagery](https://www.quanser.com/products/rotary-inverted-pendulum/) as a visual reference. No photograph or branding is included.

Read the three lower views from left to right. **Top** shows the fixed arm reference and sharp allowed arm-travel sector. **Side** shows full passive rotation in the arm-normal plane, with downward and upright marked. **Front** shows exact upright rest, with zero upright error and zero angular speeds. Hard design limits and the ideal goal are deliberately shown; numerical penalties and the finite-tolerance assessment remain described in the manuscript.

The figure uses the same black Arial text, two font sizes and Morandi palette as Figure 1. Blue identifies the driven arm; orange identifies the exact upright reference. Structural face shading is neutral gray. The [design contract](design_contract.md) states the purpose of each retained text group.
