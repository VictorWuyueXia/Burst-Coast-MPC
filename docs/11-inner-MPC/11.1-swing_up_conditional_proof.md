## 1. State, controller and proposed sets

\mathcal X_0=\bigcup_{j=1}^4\{x:|x_i-c_{j,i}|\le h_i,\ i=1,\ldots,4\}.

## 2. Stage-cost lower bound outside capture

The implemented stage objective is

$$
q(x)=\left(\frac{\theta}{135\pi/180}\right)^2+\left(\frac{\beta}{0.15}\right)^2+0.25\omega^2+0.25\nu^2,\qquad \ell(x,a)=q(x)+a^2.
$$

Outside $\mathbf D$, at least one defining bound is violated. Since all terms are nonnegative,

$$
\ell(x,a)\ge q_D:=\min\left\{(120/135)^2,\left(\frac{5\pi/180}{0.15}\right)^2,0.25(0.3)^2,0.25(0.4)^2\right\}=0.0225.
$$

The weak inequality is sufficient even though an actual threshold violation is strict. This is an analytic consequence of the stated cost and region, independent of the NN and optimizer.

## 3. Selected plan cost and progress condition

For this proof use the exact nominal ODE flow $f$ over a command interval, even when evaluating commands chosen by the numerical implementation. Let $U_k=(a_{0|k},\ldots,a_{9|k})$ denote the accepted plan at decision $k$, and $x_{i+1|k}=f(x_{i|k},a_{i|k})$, with $x_{0|k}=x_k$.

$$
J_k=\sum_{i=0}^{9}\ell(x_{i|k},a_{i|k})+F(x_{10|k}),\qquad
F(x)=\max_{j=1,2}[-Q_j(o(x),\pi(o(x)))].
$$

Here $o$ is the actual scaled observation, $Q_j$ are the frozen critics and $\pi$ is the frozen actor. Define the exact accounting residual

$$
r_k=J_{k+1}-J_k+\ell(x_k,a_{0|k}).
$$

The required condition is a verified uniform bound

$$
r_k\le\bar r<q_D\quad\text{before first capture}.
$$

Then $\delta=q_D-\bar r>0$ gives $J_{k+1}\le J_k-\delta$ outside capture. The discussed value $\bar r=0.01$ would give $\delta=0.0125$, but is only illustrative. It has not been measured, justified or certified.

If a shifted candidate is feasible, use it to diagnose the residual. Let $z=x_{10|k}$ and append terminal action $\kappa(z)$, initially the frozen actor. Define

$$
d(z)=\ell(z,\kappa(z))+F(f(z,\kappa(z)))-F(z).
$$

Let $s_k=J(x_{k+1},U_{k+1})-J(x_{k+1},U_k^{\rm shift})$. Under exact common dynamics and tail construction, cancellation gives $r_k=d(z)+s_k$. The actual implementation has different seed, prediction and execution resolutions; do not assume this cancellation is exact for archived objective values. Bound the discrepancy or evaluate the direct residual consistently.

## 4. Lemma: the exact nominal plan cost has a finite lower bound

### 4.1 Growth of the frozen networks

For a finite SiLU network, $|\operatorname{SiLU}(s)|\le|s|$ and every affine layer satisfies $\|Ah+b\|\le\|A\|\|h\|+\|b\|$. Recursing through finitely many layers yields an at-most-linear bound in input magnitude. The actor output is bounded by tanh. Sine and cosine observations are bounded; terminal arm angle is bounded for feasible plans.

Consequently, for finite weights and finite nonzero observation divisors there exist computable $A_F,B_F\ge0$ such that

$$
F(x)\ge-A_F-B_F\|v\|,\qquad v=(\omega,\nu)^\mathsf T.
$$

Use a common bound for both critics. No differentiability at a critic tie is needed. The weights are required to obtain useful numerical constants.

### 4.2 Mechanical energy and velocity growth

The source uses zero damping and

$$
M(\alpha)=\begin{bmatrix}A+J_p\sin^2\alpha&C\cos\alpha\\C\cos\alpha&J_p\end{bmatrix},\qquad
E=\tfrac12v^\mathsf TM(\alpha)v+G(1-\cos\alpha).
$$

Values derived from the inspected physical configuration are

$$
A=0.0004021916667,\quad J_p=0.000133128,\quad C=0.00013158\quad[\mathrm{kg\,m^2}],\qquad G=0.01518588\quad[\mathrm{N\,m}].
$$

Here $A$ is base inertia, $J_p$ pendulum inertia about its pivot, $C$ coupling inertia, and $G$ gravity torque coefficient. The determinant obeys

$$
\det M\ge AJ_p-C^2=3.62296758\times10^{-8}>0.
$$

Since $\operatorname{tr}M\le A+2J_p$, valid uniform eigenvalue bounds are

$$
m=\frac{AJ_p-C^2}{A+2J_p}\approx5.41997\times10^{-5},\qquad \bar m=A+2J_p\approx6.68448\times10^{-4},\qquad mI\preceq M\preceq\bar mI.
$$

These rounded numbers are descriptive; formal numerical verification should use outward-rounded bounds. Their units are kg m squared.

The energy identity is $\dot E=u\omega$, hence

$$
\dot E\le u_{\max}\sqrt{2E/m}.
$$

By integration, with the zero-energy case handled by a limiting regularization,

$$
\sqrt{E(T)}\le\sqrt{E(0)}+\frac{u_{\max}T}{\sqrt{2m}}.
$$

Using $E(0)\le\bar m\|v(0)\|^2/2+2G$ and $E(T)\ge m\|v(T)\|^2/2$ gives

$$
\|v(T)\|\le b_1\|v(0)\|+b_0,\qquad
b_1=\sqrt{\bar m/m},\quad b_0=2\sqrt{G/m}+u_{\max}T/m.
$$

Here $T=N\Delta=1$ s. Thus terminal speed grows at most linearly with current speed over the fixed horizon. This is an exact ODE statement; it is not automatically a property of unrestricted fixed-step RK4 at arbitrary velocities.

### 4.3 Completing the square

Set $c_0=A_F+B_Fb_0$ and $c_1=B_Fb_1$. Then $F(x_{10|k})\ge-c_0-c_1\|v_k\|$. The first stage contributes $0.25\|v_k\|^2$ and all other stage terms are nonnegative. In the numerical units of the implemented cost,

$$
J_k\ge0.25\|v_k\|^2-c_1\|v_k\|-c_0
=\tfrac14(\|v_k\|-2c_1)^2-c_1^2-c_0
\ge J_{\min}:=-c_0-c_1^2.
$$

Therefore a finite lower bound exists for feasible exact nominal plans. It does not require positive definiteness of $F$, convergence of the actor, or a bounded velocity assumption. Numerical evaluation of this bound requires the actual checkpoint; a tighter regional bound may be much more useful.

## 5. Conditional finite time capture theorem

Assume:

1. Every allowed initial state and controller initialization admits an accepted plan. Before first capture, execution continues with feasible plans and a well-defined nominal trajectory; constraints hold at the claimed temporal resolution.
2. The preceding lower bound applies to every selected plan before capture and the next plan used in the residual comparison.
3. The uniform residual inequality $r_k\le\bar r<q_D$ holds whenever the current state has not yet reached $\mathbf D$.
4. These statements cover the specified solver histories and random outcomes. If randomness is fixed, state precisely the seed, batch layout, and initialization scope.

Let $K_D=\inf\{k\ge0:x_k\in\mathbf D\}$ and $\delta=q_D-\bar r$. If $x_0\in\mathbf D$, then $K_D=0$. Otherwise, supposing capture has not occurred at indices $0,\ldots,K-1$ gives

$$
J_K\le J_0-K\delta.
$$

Since $J_K\ge J_{\min}$, such a trajectory cannot exist for $K>(J_0-J_{\min})/\delta$. Thus

$$
K_D\le\left\lfloor\frac{J_0-J_{\min}}{\delta}\right\rfloor+1<\infty.
$$

The physical entry-time bound is $\Delta K_D$. If $J_0\le J_{\max}$ uniformly over the allowed initial set and initialization, replacing $J_0$ by $J_{\max}$ gives a uniform bound. Compactness of the initial set alone does not prove feasibility or continuity of the sampled controller; establish the required bound on accepted initial plans explicitly.

This proves capture, not invariance of $\mathbf D$, safety without the stated feasibility assumption, or subsequent convergence into $\Omega$.

## 7. Validation procedure and alternatives

1. Reconstruct the objective from stage states, actions and terminal critics; verify execution continuity and settings. Preserve original run identity and seeds.
2. Calculate first simultaneous entry into the full $\mathbf D$ for each trajectory. Report nonentries explicitly. Check both command instants and dense trajectories, distinguishing the claims.
3. Calculate direct residuals before capture; report maxima, quantiles and worst cases. Separate terminal defect, selection gap, and numerical mismatch where possible.
4. If individual residuals fail the threshold, test block progress: for a chosen integer $m>0$, require $J_{k+m}-J_k\le-\delta_m$ whenever the entire block avoids $\mathbf D$, with $\delta_m>0$. Preserve feasibility and lower bounds; inspect intermediate entry rather than only block endpoints.
5. Search adversarial initial states in each box, including boundaries, and relevant solver histories. Sampling detects violations but does not certify unsampled points.
6. Prove coverage with interval or derivative bounds, subdivision, or verified reachable sets. NN smoothness does not remove CEM elite-selection and audit discontinuities.
7. If a scalar progress certificate remains too conservative, propagate verified initial boxes to capture directly. If only a subset can be certified, define it explicitly and report excluded states.
8. Address controller memory when handing captured trajectories to the holding proof. A fresh initialization proof on $\mathbf D$ is insufficient if inherited stored plans are not covered.
