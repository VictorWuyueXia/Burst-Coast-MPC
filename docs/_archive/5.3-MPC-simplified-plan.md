## Simplified formulation

The three term structure is good as a minimal experiment. Use this:

$$
J =
\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
\left[
w_E e_{E,k}^2
+
w_L g_{l,k}^2
\frac{e_{L,k}^2}{e_{E,k}^2+\epsilon_E^2}
+
w_u \bar{u}_k^2
+
w_{\Delta u}\bar{\Delta u}_k^2
\right]
+
J_T .
$$

For the first implementation, omit terminal cost until the stage behavior is verified.

---

## 1. Mathematical definition

State:

$$
x =
\begin{bmatrix}
\theta \\
\omega
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Energy:

$$
K = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2,
\qquad
U = mgl(1 + \cos\theta),
\qquad
E = K + U.
$$

Upright target energy:

$$
E^\star = 2mgl.
$$

Normalized energy error:

$$
e_E = \frac{E-E^\star}{E^\star}.
$$

Angular momentum about the pivot:

$$
L = I\omega.
$$

Target momentum:

$$
L^\star = 0.
$$

Use a normalized momentum error:

$$
e_L = \frac{L-L^\star}{I\omega_n}
= \frac{\omega}{\omega_n},
\qquad
\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{mgl}{I}}.
$$

Local upright gate:

$$
g_l =
\exp
\left(
-\frac{\theta_w^2}{\sigma_\theta^2}
\right),
$$

where

$$
\theta_w = \operatorname{wrap}_{[-\pi,\pi]}(\theta - \theta^\star),
\qquad
\theta^\star = 0.
$$

Input normalization:

$$
\bar{u} = \frac{u}{u_{\max}},
$$

$$
\bar{\Delta u} = \frac{u_k-u_{k-1}}{u_{\max}}.
$$

Final stage cost:

$$
\ell_k =
w_E e_E^2
+
w_L g_l^2
\frac{e_L^2}{e_E^2+\epsilon_E^2}
+
w_u \bar{u}^2
+
w_{\Delta u}\bar{\Delta u}^2.
$$

This creates the intended logic:

When energy is wrong, the energy term dominates.

When energy is nearly correct and the pendulum is near the upright angle, the momentum term becomes strong.

When actuation is unnecessary, the input term prefers zero torque.

## 2. Recommended starting weights

Use conservative values first:

```yaml
mpc:
  cost:
    w-energy: 1.0
    w-momentum: 0.02
    w-input: 1.0e-3
    w-delta-input: 1.0e-3
    w-terminal: 10.0

    epsilon-energy: 0.05
    sigma-theta-rad: 0.35
```

Reasoning:

$$
\epsilon_E = 0.05
$$

means the momentum term becomes strong when energy error is within roughly 5 percent.

At the upright with

$$
e_E = 0,\qquad e_L = 0.1,
$$

the momentum feature becomes:

$$
\frac{0.1^2}{0.05^2} = 4.
$$

With

$$
w_L = 0.02,
$$

the weighted momentum cost is:

$$
0.08.
$$

That is strong enough to damp local angular velocity, but not so large that it destroys swing up.

If the system still passes through the upright too fast, increase:

```yaml
w-momentum: 0.05
```

If it stops pumping energy too early, reduce:

```yaml
w-momentum: 0.01
```

## 3. MPC behavior plan

### Energy pumping region

When

$$
|e_E| \gg \epsilon_E,
$$

the momentum term is suppressed by the denominator:

$$
\frac{1}{e_E^2+\epsilon_E^2}.
$$

The optimizer mainly minimizes:

$$
w_E e_E^2.
$$

Since

$$
\dot{E} = \omega u,
$$

the expected action is:

$$
u \approx u_{\max} \operatorname{sign}(\omega)
\quad
\text{when}
\quad
E < E^\star,
$$

and

$$
u \approx -u_{\max} \operatorname{sign}(\omega)
\quad
\text{when}
\quad
E > E^\star.
$$

### Capture region

When

$$
|e_E| \approx 0,
\qquad
|\theta_w| \lesssim \sigma_\theta,
$$

the momentum term activates. The optimizer is pushed toward:

$$
\omega \rightarrow 0.
$$

This directly targets the desired top capture condition:

$$
\theta = 0,
\qquad
\omega = 0,
\qquad
E = E^\star.
$$

### Input economy

The input term makes the optimizer prefer zero torque unless torque materially improves energy or momentum.

This is consistent with your burst coast idea. It removes the previous saturation seeking behavior.

## 4. Event based replanning

Use section crossing events:

$$
\theta = n\pi,
\qquad n \in \mathbb{Z}.
$$

The simulator should keep an internal unwrapped angle:

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{raw}}.
$$

Before publishing observation, wrap only the observed angle:

$$
\theta_{\mathrm{obs}} = \operatorname{wrap}_{[-\pi,\pi]}(\theta_{\mathrm{raw}}).
$$

The controller replans when:

```text
active plan is missing
or active plan is exhausted
or theta_raw crosses n*pi
```

This gives physically meaningful replanning points:

$$
\theta = 0
\quad
\text{top crossing},
$$

$$
\theta = \pi
\quad
\text{bottom crossing}.
$$

## 5. Dev plan

### Step 1: Replace feature module

Remove active phase proxy cost. Add:

```python
normalized_energy_error(state, pendulum)

normalized_momentum_error(state, pendulum)
energy_momentum_value(state, pendulum, cost)
```

Keep NumPy and CasADi implementations aligned.

### Step 2: Replace CasADi cost

Current symbolic value function should become:

```python
def energy_momentum_value(x, pendulum, cost):
    e_E = normalized_energy_error(x, pendulum)
    e_L = normalized_momentum_error(x, pendulum)
    g_l = local_upright_gate(x, pendulum, cost)

    return (
        cost.w_energy * e_E**2
        + cost.w_momentum * g_l**2 * e_L**2 / (e_E**2 + cost.epsilon_energy**2)
    )
```

Burst stage cost becomes:

```python
value = energy_momentum_value(x, pendulum, cost)
value += cost.w_input * normalized_u**2
value += cost.w_delta_input * normalized_delta_u**2
```

## 6. Potential flaws

### 1.  The momentum term can create a “do not reach target energy” artifact

Because the momentum penalty grows when $e_E \to 0$, the optimizer might prefer keeping a small energy error to avoid paying a large momentum penalty.

The regularization $\epsilon_E$ reduces this, but does not remove it completely. If this appears, replace the inverse energy factor with a bounded gate:

$$
g_E =
\exp
\left(
- \frac{e_E^2}{\sigma_E^2}
\right),
$$

and use:

$$
w_L g_l^2 g_E e_L^2.
$$

That is numerically cleaner, but less aggressive.

### 3. A theta only local gate can activate during fast top crossing

At

$$
\theta \approx 0
$$

but large $|\omega|$, the local gate is large. That is intended, because it activates braking. However, it may become too aggressive and fight necessary pass through motion before there is enough energy.

The inverse energy factor helps by requiring small $|e_E|$, but if the pendulum often crosses near the top with low energy, use a narrower gate:

```yaml
sigma-theta-rad: 0.20
```
### 4. Event replanning at every $n\pi$ crossing may be too sparse

For early tests, this is clean. But if the controller commits to a bad plan between crossings, it may not recover until the next section event.

A practical safeguard is:

```text
replan on section crossing or active plan exhausted
```

Later, add deviation based early wake, but not in this simplified version.
