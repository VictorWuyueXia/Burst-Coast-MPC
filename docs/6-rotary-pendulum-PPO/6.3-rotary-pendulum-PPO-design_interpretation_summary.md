# Rotary-Pendulum PPO Design: Interpretation Summary

## Recommendation

Start with PPO controlling the bounded shaft torque directly at 100 Hz. Keep the
existing RK4 plant at 500 Hz by holding each PPO action for five physics steps.

Do not make the first PPO agent choose the MPC horizon. That would place repeated
nonlinear MPC solves inside training and turn the rollout into a variable-duration
semi-Markov problem. Direct torque is the smallest reliable experiment and still
learns the state-value critic needed by the larger project.

## Network

Use one shared network with about nine thousand parameters:

```text
8 physical features -> 64 -> 64 -> actor head 32 -> torque mean
                              `-> critic head 32 -> state value
```

The eight inputs preserve unwrapped arm travel, encode pendulum angle periodically,
normalize both velocities by physical scales, and expose swing energy, phase, and
previous torque. A tanh-squashed Gaussian enforces the torque limit exactly.

## Reward judgment

The reward has one primary job: reduce a bounded task potential from the current state
to the next state. This rewards energy progress without penalizing the unavoidable
low-energy beginning of swing-up.

A weaker phase term asks arm acceleration to pump or remove pendulum energy with the
correct sign. Small torque-change, effort, and cable-boundary terms break near-ties.
Success and failure receive explicit terminal bonuses.

The reward is intentionally not an accumulated squared energy error. That rejected
formulation would teach the agent that every early swing-up state is intrinsically bad.

## Training judgment

Train in three iterations: balance near upright, learn mixed-phase energy acquisition,
then refine from exact downward rest. Use 64 vectorized environments and select the
best deterministic validation checkpoint rather than the final checkpoint.

The main pass condition is at least 95% exact-downward success without cable-limit
violations, median success within 5 s, plus a stricter 0.25 s upright-dwell diagnostic.
Only two reward coefficients receive small planned sweeps; network width is not a
first-line tuning parameter.

## Connection to MPC

After validation, freeze the encoder and critic. The negative critic value can be
scaled into an MPC terminal cost, and zero-torque coast rollouts can be ranked by the
same value. This is the cleanest bridge from the PPO proof of concept to learned
terminal value and later horizon selection.
